Our Story

A small room, a good cup, an afternoon well spent.

Meraki is a neighbourhood specialty coffee shop. White walls, cane chairs, round wooden tables, and a bakery counter that fills the room with the smell of fresh croissants.

We pour Vietnamese iced coffee, Spanish lattes and a serious flat white. We bake Korean cheese buns, cinnamon rolls and warm chocolate chunk cookies. And we keep things quiet enough that you can hear yourself think.
