Our Story

One menu. Many destinations.

Nuevatown is a cafe & lounge in New Town built around a simple idea — a single menu that takes you around the world. Italian today, Asian tomorrow, classic continental for breakfast, sundaes after dinner.

Bright, comfortable seating. Friendly service. And portions made to share. Pull up a chair, order one of everything, stay a while.
