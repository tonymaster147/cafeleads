Our Story

The cafe with its feet in the water.

Herbs Cafe was built around one thing: the lake. The dining room sits inside a high-ceilinged greenhouse with sunken concrete seats around still water pools. The bamboo deck reaches out over the lake itself — string lights, rainbow umbrellas, and dinner with herons for neighbours.

Our menu wanders too. Bengali thalis on banana leaves at lunch. Wood-fired pizzas and creamy pastas at dinner. Hand-pulled lattes any time. Stay through sundown — that's when it gets magical.

— The kind of place you remember the smell of, weeks later.

"Sit by the water, listen to the breeze, eat slowly. There is nowhere else you need to be."

— What we tell every guest at the door
