Our Story

The same recipe, for three generations.

Started in 1968 as a small breakfast counter, Garden Cafe has grown into one of Kolkata's most-loved family restaurants. What hasn't changed: the stone-ground batter, the curry-leaf-tempered sambar, the coconut chutney made fresh every morning.

Our menu cover still carries the same hand-illustrated South Indian platter our founders chose. Some things, you just don't update.

"The first dosa was rolled in 1968. The recipe is the same. So is the welcome."
