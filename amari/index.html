Our Story

Amari was born from a love for slow mornings, well-pulled espresso and the smell of fresh sourdough out of the oven. We are a specialty coffee bar, a full eating house and an artisanal bakery — all under one roof.

Every coffee we serve is roasted in small batches. Every loaf, croissant and pastry is shaped by hand. Come for the coffee, stay for the food, leave with a box of bakes.
