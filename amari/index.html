Our Story

A coffee bar, a bakery and a dining room — all at once.

Amari is built around three simple things: a serious specialty coffee program, an artisanal bakery turning out bread and bakes daily, and a kitchen that serves real food, all day long.

The room is warm — terracotta walls, wooden tables, soft lighting, the hum of a pour-over kettle and the smell of something just out of the oven. Stay as long as you'd like.
