Our Story

A small bakery, with a big heart.

Cafe 14 is the bakery cafe you wish your block had — Edison-bulb umbrellas outside, white lattice chairs, bookshelves inside, and a glass case full of whatever we baked this morning.

We bake cakes for your celebrations, fold pastries for your tea breaks, grill sandwiches for your lunch hour, and stay open late so you have somewhere to land after dinner.
