About Us

A New Town favourite for every kind of meal.

Coffee House in New Town is a multi-cuisine restaurant where you can swing in for a quick coffee and grilled sandwich, settle in for a long lunch, or come up to the rooftop for an evening of food and conversation.

Bright dining room, formal seating, a full bar of beverages and a menu that spans Indian, Chinese and continental — all in one place.
