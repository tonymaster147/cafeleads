Our Story

Big food.Tiny prices.

Baithak is your friendly drive-in adda — wicker chairs spilling onto the street, paper lanterns overhead, and a kitchen serving everything from a hot plate of chowmein to a creamy white sauce pasta.

We started with one simple idea: great street-style food shouldn't cost a fortune. Breakfast starts at ₹40. Combos go up to ₹150. And the vibe? Always free.
