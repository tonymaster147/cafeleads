Our Story

An ode to Ray, in every cup.

L'RAY Treat is a Baker's Café that lives and breathes the world of Satyajit Ray — his films, his sketches, his Kolkata. Walk in and you'll find framed stills on the walls, hand-drawn Apu & Feluda on the mugs, and the unhurried hum of a good adda.

We bake fresh every morning, brew our coffee strong, and plate Bengali-meets-continental favourites that make every visit feel like a scene worth remembering.
