Our Story

The neighbourhood's after-hours hangout.

Buddy's Yard is where New Town comes to slow down — a café and lounge with moody lighting, a hand-painted city-skyline mural and pastas that arrive bubbling hot.

Whether you're here to work late on your laptop, catch up with friends over coffee, or just escape the mall buzz for a bit — pull up a chair. We've saved one for you.
